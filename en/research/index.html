Research

Four connected research directions define my work in skin biology and translational cosmetic biotechnology.

01

Acne & Skin Microbiome

We study microbial ecology, skin and gut–skin interactions, inflammation and treatment response in acne, with particular interest in how metabolic and dietary factors alter disease biology.

02

Probiotics & Postbiotics

Our work evaluates beneficial bacteria and their cell-free products for antimicrobial activity, inflammatory control and translational skin applications.

03

Natural Products & Cosmetic Biotechnology

We investigate natural materials and fermentation-derived actives for anti-inflammatory, barrier-supporting and cosmetic applications, connecting mechanism-focused experiments with product-oriented translation.

04

Environmental Skin Biology

We examine how ultraviolet exposure and environmental stress alter skin-cell function, DNA-damage responses and molecular pathways relevant to photoaging and resilience.
