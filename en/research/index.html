Research

Four connected research directions define my work in skin biology and translational cosmetic biotechnology.

01

Acne & Skin Microbiome

I study how skin and gut-associated microorganisms interact with host inflammation, sebum biology and treatment response in acne, including the influence of diet, metabolites and antimicrobial pressure.

02

Probiotics & Postbiotics

My work evaluates probiotic strains and cell-free supernatants for antimicrobial, antifungal, anti-biofilm and anti-inflammatory activity, then connects these phenotypes with genomic and mechanistic evidence.

03

Natural Products & Cosmetic Biotechnology

I investigate plant-derived and fermentation-derived actives for barrier support, anti-inflammatory activity, pigmentation, photoaging and other cosmetic or dermatologic applications.

04

Environmental Skin Biology

I examine how ultraviolet radiation and environmental stressors alter DNA damage, nucleotide excision repair, inflammatory signaling and skin-cell function in cell and animal models.

Experimental pipeline

Techniques & Experimental Pipeline

A cross-disciplinary workflow spanning microbes, cells, skin models and omics-guided interpretation.

01Discovery & isolation

Lactic acid bacteria, actinomycetes, natural materials and functional candidates.

02Functional screening

Antibacterial, antifungal, MIC/MBC/MFC, biofilm, antioxidant and cytotoxicity assays.

03Skin & cell models

Keratinocytes, sebocytes, melanocyte models, UV/PM stress and host–microbe experiments.

04Mechanism validation

qPCR, Western blot, ELISA, immunofluorescence, IHC, histology and microscopy.

05Omics & imaging

Whole-genome sequencing, comparative genomics, microbiome profiling, SEM and metabolomics.

06Translational readout

Dermatology, cosmetic biotechnology, oral health and mechanism-to-product interpretation.
