Oral Lactiplantibacillus plantarum accelerates nucleotide excision repair and suppresses UVB-induced checkpoint immunosuppression

npj Biofilms and Microbiomes · 2025 JIF · released 2026

Links oral Lactiplantibacillus plantarum with accelerated nucleotide excision repair and attenuation of UVB-driven checkpoint immunosuppression.

Key data
  • Oral TH-B59 accelerated cyclobutane pyrimidine dimer clearance and reduced γ-H2AX accumulation after UVB.
  • NER-related genes including Xpc, Ercc4, Xpa and Ercc5 were restored.
  • Inflammatory and immune-checkpoint signaling was attenuated alongside changes in the gut microbiome.

2026First authorJIF 4.5

Genome guided discovery of antibiotic activity in Streptomyces virginiae THA-960 against multidrug resistant Staphylococcus aureus

iScience · 2025 JIF · released 2026

Genome-guided discovery workflow linking comparative genomics, biosynthetic gene clusters and validated anti-MRSA activity.

Key data
  • MICs against clinical MDR-S. aureus were as low as 0.08 mg/mL.
  • Complete genome sequencing identified 31 specialized metabolite gene clusters.
  • Phylogenomic analysis across 521 Streptomyces genomes highlighted conserved biosynthetic potential.
  • Time-kill assays, SEM and UPLC-QTOF-MS supported rapid bactericidal activity and amycomicin-linked chemistry.

2025Co-first authorJIF 5.3

Effect of Lactiplantibacillus plantarum THK-J112 against Candida overgrowth in acne complications

Probiotics and Antimicrobial Proteins · 2025 JIF · released 2026

Demonstrates antifungal, anti-adhesion and anti-inflammatory activity relevant to Candida overgrowth in acne complications.

Key data
  • Inhibition zones exceeded 10 mm against Candida tropicalis KCTC 17762.
  • Minimum fungicidal concentration was 2.5 mg/mL.
  • THK-J112 CFS reduced Candida adhesion to epithelial cells by up to 80.25%.
  • TNF-α decreased by 30.58%, VEGF by 78.25%, and H2O2 reached 90.43 μM per 1 g/mL CFS.
