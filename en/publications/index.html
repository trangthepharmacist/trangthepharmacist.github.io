Genome guided discovery of antibiotic activity in Streptomyces virginiae THA-960 against multidrug resistant Staphylococcus aureus

iScience · 2025 JIF

Genome-guided discovery workflow linking comparative genomics, biosynthetic gene clusters and validated anti-MRSA activity.

Key data
  • MICs against clinical MDR-S. aureus were as low as 0.08 mg/mL.
  • Complete genome sequencing identified 31 specialized metabolite gene clusters.
  • Phylogenomic analysis across 521 Streptomyces genomes highlighted conserved amycomicin biosynthetic potential.
  • Time-kill assays, SEM and UPLC-QTOF-MS supported rapid bactericidal activity and amycomicin-linked chemistry.

Oral Lactiplantibacillus plantarum accelerates nucleotide excision repair and suppresses UVB-induced checkpoint immunosuppression

npj Biofilms and Microbiomes · 2025 JIF

Combines UVB cell and mouse data to show enhanced CPD repair, reduced γ-H2AX accumulation, and remodeling of UVB-driven inflammatory checkpoint programs after oral TH-B59 treatment.

Key data
  • In UVB-irradiated BALB/c skin, oral TH-B59 accelerated CPD clearance and reduced γ-H2AX accumulation.
  • NER-related genes including Xpc, Ercc4, Xpa and Ercc5 were restored alongside attenuation of PI3K/AKT signaling.
  • The study also showed suppression of NF-κB/COX-2/PGE2 and PD-L1, with increased IL-12 expression.
  • Integrated microbiome analysis linked TH-B59-associated gut shifts to improved skin repair and immune surveillance.

Effect of Lactiplantibacillus plantarum THK-J112 Against Candida Overgrowth in Acne Complications

Probiotics and Antimicrobial Proteins · 2025 JIF

Demonstrates broad antifungal activity, reduced Candida adhesion, anti-inflammatory effects, and ultrastructural disruption relevant to acne-associated fungal overgrowth.

Key data
  • Broad-spectrum antifungal activity showed inhibition zones greater than 10 mm against Candida tropicalis KCTC 17762.
  • Minimum fungicidal concentration was 2.5 mg/mL.
  • THK-J112 CFS reduced Candida adhesion to epithelial cells by up to 80.25%, exceeding Amphotericin B in the adhesion assay.
  • TNF-α decreased by 30.58%, VEGF by 78.25%, and H2O2 reached 90.43 μM per 1 g/mL CFS.

2024Co-first authorJIF 5.9

The Potential Teeth Bleaching and Halitosis Prevention Effects of Pediococcus inopinatus THK-30, a Kimchi-Derived Lactic Acid Bacterium: In Vitro Study

Journal of Functional Biomaterials · 2025 JIF

Mechanistic work linking THK-30 antibacterial activity with biofilm inhibition, artificial-tooth bleaching, and halitosis-related oral pathogen control.

Key data
  • THK-30 showed activity against Streptococcus mutans, Porphyromonas gingivalis and Fusobacterium nucleatum.
  • A synergistic antibacterial effect was observed when THK-30 was combined with 8% sodium hexametaphosphate (SHMP).
  • Artificial-tooth bleaching was assessed using the CIEDE2000 colorimetric method.
  • Cell-free supernatant significantly inhibited biofilm formation on artificial teeth, both alone and with SHMP.
