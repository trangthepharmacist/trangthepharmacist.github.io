Research profile

Trang Nguyen, Ph.D.

I am a Research Professor at Kyung Hee University working across skin microbiome science, dermatology and cosmetic biotechnology. My research asks how microorganisms, microbial metabolites, natural compounds and environmental stress reshape skin inflammation, barrier function, DNA-damage responses and therapeutic outcomes.

I completed my Ph.D. at Kyung Hee University in August 2026 after earning an M.Sc. there in 2024. My earlier training is in pharmacy, with a B.Pharm. from Ton Duc Thang University in 2020.

Scientific focus

Mechanisms with translational direction

My work links mechanistic experiments in skin biology with questions that matter for dermatology, microbiome science and cosmetic biotechnology.

Experimental pipeline

Techniques & Experimental Pipeline

01Discovery & isolation

Lactic acid bacteria, actinomycetes, natural materials and functional candidates.

02Functional screening

Antibacterial, antifungal, MIC/MBC/MFC, biofilm, antioxidant and cytotoxicity assays.

03Skin & cell models

Keratinocytes, sebocytes, melanocyte models, UV/PM stress and host–microbe experiments.

04Mechanism validation

qPCR, Western blot, ELISA, immunofluorescence, IHC, histology and microscopy.

05Omics & imaging

Whole-genome sequencing, comparative genomics, microbiome profiling, SEM and metabolomics.

06Translational readout

Dermatology, cosmetic biotechnology, oral health and mechanism-to-product interpretation.
