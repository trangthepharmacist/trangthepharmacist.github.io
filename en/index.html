About

Trang Nguyen, Ph.D.

I am a Research Professor at Kyung Hee University working at the intersection of skin microbiome science, dermatology and cosmetic biotechnology. My research examines how microorganisms, natural compounds, microbial metabolites and environmental stressors influence skin inflammation, barrier function and therapeutic response.
